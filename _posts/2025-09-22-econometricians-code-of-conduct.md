title: Personal Code of Conduct for Econometrics
---

1) Communication & Teaching 
Statisticians and econometricians are constrained by their ultimate step of teaching the insights/products of their analysis to their audience. Communicaitng economic insights is lesson planning in miniature. First and foremost, you have to learn to be an adequate teacher and science communicator
- Tailoring explanations to your audience
- Refine communication via feedback and iteration
- Use simple, precise language rather than jargon
- Organize ideas hierarchically and logically.
- Be explicit about assumptions, uncertainty, and limitations.
- Highlight the main messages, particularly actionable insights if you want change.

2) Societal Relevance
I want to help analyze and educate about problems that matter to everday life: climate change, infrastructure, cost of living, education, labor and jobs, mental health, equalizing opportunity, strengthening community and social capital, relationships, and sustainable agriculture. 
I lean toward applied analysis—using existing theory effectively rather than always inventing new theory.

3) Collboration & Systems Thinking
- Approach problems from mulitple perspectives
- Working across disciplines is a natural and easy way to promote a systems perspective. 
- Don't learn everything; build a network of complementary experts. 

4) When Not to Use Analysis
- Overthinking: the problem is simple and the answer is already visible.
- Avoidance: you’re resisting collecting data or taking real-world action.
- Ethical evasion: hiding behind “neutrality” when moral assumptions are implicit.
- Time pressure: when urgency makes a quick, approximate answer more valuable than a perfect one.

5) Good Modeling Principles
- Interpretability is just as much a factor of accuracy. 
- Accept that all models are wrong; some are useful. Know the limits of yours from the start.
- Don’t reinvent the wheel—review existing models and literature.
- Breaking down the problem into tractable steps; try building models with qualitative descriptions of key variables, components, and relationships.
- Clarify your research question far more than you think necessary. 

6) Design Thinking
- Understand your client deeply--working with someone in mind. 
- Follow interative design frameworks like double diamond: explore, define, develop, deliver
- take inspiration from the engineering design cycle

7) Standards & Reality Checks
- Double check modeling coherence (logical, consistent, and understandable) and correspondence (close similarity, connection, or equivalence to the real world). 
- Prioritize reproducibility, transparency, and self-contained analyses--including full code documentation, data when possible, and references
- Maintain data confidentiality, security, and stewardship to preserve public trust.
